The Business

Big Data, Bigger Bags

A creator-management and brand-partnership agency connecting brands with high-reach YouTube creators, musicians, and celebrity channels — operating in a market where subscriber counts are easy to inflate but genuine engagement is hard to fake, and brands increasingly demand proof of ROI before committing a budget.

Financial

Revenue per 1,000 views, revenue mix by stream, conversion rate on monetized posts.

Customer

High-value engagement rate, repeat viewer rate, sponsored vs. organic growth.

Process

Posting adherence, production time, the Partnership Readiness Index.

Learning & Growth

Analytics training, negotiation skill, campaign-system usage.

The Series

Six projects, in order

Each project builds directly on the last — the KPIs defined in Project 1 become the thresholds in Project 2, which become the fields in Project 3's CRM, which get tested against real funnel data in Projects 4–5, and synthesized into a governed AI layer in Project 6.

Project 6 · Flagship

ConversionIQ

A Monday-morning intelligence assistant, not an autonomous decision-maker. It synthesizes Tableau, Salesforce, and Google Analytics into one brief — then answers follow-up questions using only the data already in that brief.

ConversionIQ
Two modes: an automated Monday report, and a conversational follow-up that never leaves the data it was given.
